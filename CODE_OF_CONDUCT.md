# Code of Conduct

The **Pixel — Image Extractor** community is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or level of technical experience.

By participating in this project in any capacity—whether reporting an issue, contributing code, submitting documentation, or participating in discussions—you agree to abide by the principles outlined in this Code of Conduct.

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, respectful, and encouraging community, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in **Pixel — Image Extractor** a safe and enriching experience for all individuals, regardless of:

- Age, physical appearance, or body size
- Visible or invisible disabilities and neurodiversity
- Ethnicity, nationality, race, or caste
- Gender identity, expression, and sex characteristics
- Sexual orientation
- Level of experience, education, or socio-economic status
- Religion, philosophical beliefs, or lack thereof

We are committed to building a cooperative, empathetic, and constructive development culture.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a thriving and collaborative community include:

- **Demonstrating Empathy:** Treating every contributor and user with patience, kindness, and respect.
- **Using Inclusive Language:** Choosing language that is welcoming, professional, and non-exclusionary.
- **Constructive Engagement:** Welcoming differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback gracefully, and offering well-reasoned, actionable critiques.
- **Community-First Mindset:** Prioritizing the overall health, performance, and accessibility of the project over individual preferences.
- **Mentorship & Guidance:** Supporting new contributors and answering questions with clarity and patience.

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited within any project space:

- **Harassment & Abuse:** Offensive comments, derogatory jokes, trolling, personal insults, or political attacks.
- **Sexualized Behavior:** The use of sexualized language, imagery, or unwarranted sexual advances.
- **Doxxing & Privacy Violations:** Publishing another person's private details (real name, physical address, email, phone number) without explicit permission.
- **Sustained Disruption:** Repeatedly derailing issues, pull requests, or discussions with off-topic or hostile commentary.
- **Advocating Harm:** Encouraging or condoning harm towards any individual or group.
- **Unprofessional Conduct:** Any action that would be deemed inappropriate or harmful in a professional open-source environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces directly associated with the **Pixel — Image Extractor** project, including:

- The GitHub repository (issues, discussions, pull requests, commit messages, and release notes).
- Project documentation, web pages, and associated community channels.
- Direct communication (emails, chat messages) between contributors and maintainers regarding project matters.

It also applies when an individual represents the project in public spaces, online forums, conferences, or social media.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers, led by [`@ichshakib`](https://github.com/ichshakib), are responsible for clarifying and upholding these community standards. Maintainers have the authority and duty to:

- Review, edit, moderate, or remove comments, commits, pull requests, and issues that violate this Code of Conduct.
- Address reported incidents promptly, objectively, and confidentially.
- Temporarily or permanently suspend any contributor whose behavior violates community safety or standards.

## Reporting & Enforcement

### How to Report

If you experience, witness, or are informed of unacceptable behavior, please report the incident directly to the project maintainer:

- **Maintainer:** Shakib Khan ([@ichshakib](https://github.com/ichshakib))
- **Email:** [ichshakib@gmail.com](mailto:ichshakib@gmail.com)
- **GitHub Profile:** [`https://github.com/ichshakib`](https://github.com/ichshakib)

When submitting a report, please include as much context as possible:
1. A description of the incident and what occurred.
2. Links to relevant comments, pull requests, or commit hashes (or screenshots if applicable).
3. Any additional context that may assist the investigation.

### Investigation & Privacy

All reports will be handled with strict confidentiality. The reporter's identity will not be disclosed without explicit consent. The maintainer will investigate the situation impartially and determine appropriate next steps within a reasonable timeframe.

### Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on the severity and frequency of the violation, maintainers will follow this graduated enforcement framework:

| Level | Action | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Correction** | Private Notification | A private, written clarification detailing why the behavior was inappropriate, requesting a change in approach. |
| **2. Warning** | Formal Reprimand | A formal warning with explicit consequences for continued violations; may include temporary moderation restrictions on repository discussions. |
| **3. Temporary Suspension** | Timed Ban | A temporary ban from interacting with the repository (issues, PRs, comments) for a specified duration (e.g., 14 to 30 days). |
| **4. Permanent Exclusion** | Permanent Ban | Permanent revocation of access and interaction with all project spaces, repositories, and communications. |
